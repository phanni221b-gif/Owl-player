Neural: FSRCNNX_x2_8-0-4-1; AI Max: FSRCNNX_x2_16-0-4-1. Both run on GPU, not NPU.
Owl modifications: MAIN-hook SDR RGB-to-luma extraction and RGB reconstruction allow direct MediaCodec RGB textures without CPU readback or decoder changes. CNN weights/topology are unchanged. Original and modified shader source, license headers and SHA-256 manifests are included in the APK and source repository. The modified shaders remain LGPL-3.0-or-later.

The shaders run only when BOTH output axes exceed input by 1.3x. They are not used for HDR. The 16-channel model has greater capacity, not a measured guarantee of better quality for every clip. Fold device image quality, speed, heat and power need physical-device verification.
No Qualcomm/QNN/NPU runtime is bundled or claimed.
